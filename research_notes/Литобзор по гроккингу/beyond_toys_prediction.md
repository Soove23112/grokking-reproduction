# Grokking beyond toy algorithmic tasks, and early prediction / progress measures of grokking

Positioning target (own finding, F1): 1-layer transformer, a+b mod 113 (Nanda et al. 2023 reproduction). The eventual key Fourier frequencies are already the largest W_E components by epoch ~60–100, while the test jump happens at epochs 3,400–11,100 (i.e. the signal is available at roughly 0.5–3% of the time-to-jump). Top-4 W_E frequencies at epoch 100 contain >=3 of the final key frequencies in 4/5 seeds. MLP neurons commit to frequencies only near the jump (mean neuron FVE 0.07 -> 0.16 @ep3000 -> 0.6 @ep7000 -> 0.9).

Evidence-level tags used below: [FT] = full text (HTML/PDF) read in this session; [ABS] = arXiv abstract only (claims beyond the abstract not verified); [SNIP] = search-snippet only.

## Q1. Progress measures and hidden progress (what can be measured before the test jump, and how early)

### Takeaway
Many continuous measures rise or fall before the test jump, but almost all of them (a) are reported on modular arithmetic / S5 / small sequence tasks, (b) fire only ~1k steps or a few-to-tens of percent of the budget ahead of the jump, and (c) predict *whether/when* grokking happens, not *which* features will win. The earliest published predictor in time is Notsawo et al. (first ~400 steps of training-loss oscillations), but it reports no quantitative accuracy and predicts only grok/no-grok.

### Cited Findings

Foundational hidden-progress / progress-measure papers
- Nanda, Chan, Lieberum, Smith, Steinhardt, "Progress measures for grokking via mechanistic interpretability", arXiv 2301.05217, ICLR 2023 [FT]. Mainline run (p=113, 1-layer transformer): memorization epochs 0–1.4k, circuit formation 1.4k–9.4k, cleanup 9.4k–14k; test accuracy jumps "after around 10,000 epochs"; "the circuit is formed well before grokking occurs". Progress measures: restricted loss (begins declining before test loss), excluded loss (rises during circuit formation), Gini coefficient of Fourier-basis norms of W_E / W_L (flat during memorization, "increase[s] sharply during cleanup"), sum of squared weights. Key frequencies in the final model: k in {14, 35, 41, 42, 52} (5 of 6 nonzero embedding frequencies used). The paper analyses W_E key-frequency sparsity only for the final model and does not report when the key frequencies first become visible in W_E, nor uses frequency identity as an early predictor — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.05217).
- Barak, Edelman, Goel, Kakade, Malach, Zhang, "Hidden Progress in Deep Learning: SGD Learns Parities Near the Computational Limit", arXiv 2207.08799, NeurIPS 2022 [ABS]. k-sparse parity: abrupt learning at ~n^O(k) iterations; SGD "gradually amplifies the sparse solution via a Fourier gap in the population gradient, making continual progress that is invisible to loss and error metrics" — the closest conceptual precedent for "the right Fourier components are being amplified long before accuracy moves" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08799).
- Notsawo, Zhou, Pezeshki, Rish, Dumas, "Predicting Grokking Long Before it Happens: A look into the loss landscape of models which grok", arXiv 2306.13253 (v3 Sep 2023; venue not stated on the arXiv page) [FT]. Uses spectral energy (low-pass-filtered FFT / Hjorth parameters) of the *training-loss* curve over the **first 400 steps**; example run: train acc ~100% at ~300 steps, val acc reaches the same level at ~7k steps. Tasks: modular addition (p=97) and S5 multiplication; 2-layer transformer, AdamW lr 1e-4, wd 1. Evidence is qualitative (heatmaps of spectral energy vs. eventual val accuracy across lr/wd); **no numeric prediction accuracy reported**; empirical grokking-time law t(r)=a·r^(−γ)+b with γ=7.73 (mod-add), 1.18 (S5). Predicts *whether* grokking will occur, not which features — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.13253).
- Liu, Kitouni, Nolte, Michaud, Tegmark, Williams, "Towards Understanding Grokking: An Effective Theory of Representation Learning", arXiv 2205.10343, NeurIPS 2022 [ABS]. Generalization originates from structured representations whose dynamics are predicted by an effective theory; four phases (comprehension, grokking, memorization, confusion) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.10343).

Spectral / geometric early-warning signals (2026, all on algorithmic tasks)
- Truong et al., "Spectral Entropy Collapse as a Phase Transition in Delayed Generalisation", arXiv 2604.13123 (Apr 2026, preprint) [FT]. Normalised spectral entropy of the representation covariance crosses a task-specific threshold (~0.609; 0.605 add, 0.589 mul/sub, 0.655 S5) before the test jump in 10/10 seeds, mean lead ~1,020 steps (95% CI ~890–1,140), evaluated every 200 steps. Time-to-grok power-law fit R^2 ≈ 0.54; LOO MAPE ~3.2% at the step just before grokking, but ±~6,000-step 95% interval per run. Mean grokking step ~14,260–14,360 (inconsistent between sections). A Fourier-alignment observable on the *penultimate representation* rises 0.12 (init) -> ~0.25 by end of memorization (~step 200) -> ~0.62 near grokking; corr(entropy, alignment) ≈ −0.82. Causal: a representation-mixing intervention delays grokking (~5,020 steps, p=0.044; norm-matched ~8,304 steps, p=5e-5). MLP controls: entropy collapse alone insufficient. **Does not analyse embedding frequencies over time** — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.13123).
- Wang, Ying, Kanamori, "Distributional Spectral Diagnostics for Localizing Grokking Transitions", arXiv 2605.08237 (May 2026) [ABS]. Wasserstein/quantile coordinates of observables + Hankel DMD; reconstruction residual gives run-level grok-vs-no-grok AUROC ≈ 0.93 on held-out modular-addition transformer runs; lead time reported jointly with FPR; high-residual windows show ~3x larger perturbation deviation; authors explicitly say it is "not a universal early-warning predictor"; log-probability is the best observable tested; norm signals remain strong run-level indicators — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08237).
- Golwala, "ILDR: Geometric Early Detection of Grokking", arXiv 2604.20923 (Apr 2026, single author) [ABS]. Inter/intra-class distance ratio on penultimate-layer held-out representations; threshold at 2.5x baseline; leads the transition by **9–73% of the training budget** (mod arithmetic, S5), 950 ± 250 steps over 8 seeds (CV 26%); weight norm "consistently lags"; Grokfast slow-gradient EMA unstable (std > mean lead); early stopping saves 18.6% compute — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20923).
- Xu, "Early-Warning Signals of Grokking via Loss-Landscape Geometry", arXiv 2602.16967 (Feb 2026, single author) [ABS]. Commutator defect (non-commuting gradient updates) rises well before generalization on SCAN and Dyck-1 (plus prior modular-arithmetic results); lead time follows superlinear power law (α≈1.18 SCAN, ≈1.13 Dyck); amplifying non-commutativity speeds grokking ~32% (SCAN) / ~50% (Dyck); weight-space PCA spectral concentration is *not* a universal precursor — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16967). Snippets disagree on success counts (11/12 vs 14/15 runs with positive lead) [SNIP] — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16967v1).
- Hennick, Corlouer, "From Density Matrices to Phase Transitions in Deep Learning: Spectral Early Warnings and Interpretability", arXiv 2603.29805 (Mar 2026) [ABS]. "2-datapoint reduced density matrix"; spectral heat capacity gives provable early warning of second-order transitions via critical slowing down; validated on deep linear nets, induction-head formation, grokking, emergent misalignment — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29805).

Complexity / rank / norm / information measures
- Fan, Pascanu, Jaggi, "Deep Grokking: Would Deep Neural Networks Generalize Better?", arXiv 2405.19454 (2024) [ABS]. 12-layer MLP (MNIST-style setup) groks more readily than shallow; multi-stage generalization with secondary test surge; decreasing feature rank corresponds to the overfit->generalize transition, double-descent in feature rank aligns with multi-stage generalization; argue feature rank is a better indicator than weight norm — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19454).
- DeMoss, Sapora, Foerster, Hawes, "The Complexity Dynamics of Grokking", arXiv 2412.09810, Physica D 482 (2025) 134859 [ABS]. Rate-distortion/Kolmogorov-style lossy-compression complexity rises during memorization and falls at generalization; 30–40x better compression than naive; spectral-entropy regulariser — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.09810).
- Humayun, Balestriero, Baraniuk, "Deep Networks Always Grok and Here is Why", arXiv 2402.15555, ICML 2024 [ABS]. Local complexity (density of spline linear regions) as progress measure; linear regions undergo a phase transition, migrating away from training points toward the decision boundary; grokking on CIFAR-10 CNN and ResNet on Imagenette; "delayed robustness" (adversarial) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15555).
- Liu, Zhong, Tegmark, "Grokking as Compression: A Nonlinear Complexity Perspective", arXiv 2310.05918 (2023) [ABS]. Linear mapping number (LMN) is linear in test loss during compression, unlike L2 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05918).
- Golechha, "Progress Measures for Grokking on Real-world Tasks", arXiv 2405.12755, ICML 2024 HiLD workshop [ABS]. Grokking can occur outside the expected weight-norm range; proposes activation sparsity, absolute weight entropy, approximate local circuit complexity — correlate better with grokking on real datasets than L2 — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12755).
- Clauw, Stramaglia, Marinazzo, "Information-Theoretic Progress Measures reveal Grokking is an Emergent Phase Transition", arXiv 2408.08944, ICML 2024 MI workshop [ABS]. Synergy/redundancy (higher-order MI) between neurons show distinct phases "allowing us to anticipate when it occurs" (no lead-time number in abstract) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08944).
- Furuta, Minegishi, Iwasawa, Matsuo, arXiv 2402.16726, TMLR [ABS]. New progress measures for modular arithmetic: Fourier Frequency Density and Fourier Coefficient Ratio; polynomials superpose elementary Fourier components — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.16726).
- Prakash, Martin, arXiv 2506.04434 (Jun 2025) and arXiv 2602.02859 (Feb 2026) [ABS]. HTSR layer quality α (WeightWatcher) delineates pre-grokking, grokking and a late "anti-grokking" collapse (seen after ~1e7 steps on 1k-sample MNIST MLP and mod-add transformer), heralded by α<2 and "Correlation Traps"; activation sparsity, weight entropy, circuit complexity, L2 track the first two phases but miss anti-grokking; data-free — [2506.04434](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04434), [2602.02859](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02859).
- Mallinar, Beaglehole, Zhu, Radhakrishnan, "Emergence in non-neural models: grokking modular arithmetic via AGOP", arXiv 2407.20199, ICML 2025 oral [ABS]. Grokking with Recursive Feature Machines (kernel + AGOP): the transition "cannot be predicted from the training loss, which is identically zero, nor from the test loss, which remains constant", but is determined by gradually learned block-circulant features (hidden-progress measures on features) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20199).
- Lee et al., "Grokfast", arXiv 2405.20233 (2024) [ABS]. Slow-varying (low-frequency-in-time) gradient component is the generalization-inducing one; amplifying it speeds grokking >50x on algorithmic, image, language, graph tasks — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.20233). Its EMA used as a detector is unstable across seeds per ILDR [ABS] — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.20923).

Predictors from initialisation / theory
- Kumar, Bordelon, Gershman, Pehlevan, "Grokking as the Transition from Lazy to Rich Training Dynamics", arXiv 2310.06110 (ICLR 2024) [ABS]. Key determinants: rate of feature learning (output scale) and **alignment of the initial NTK top eigenvectors with the labels** — a predictor computable at initialisation; also MNIST and one-layer transformers — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06110).
- Lyu, Jin, Li, Du, arXiv 2311.18817, ICLR 2024 [ABS]. Large init + small wd: trapped at kernel predictor, then sharp transition to min-norm/max-margin — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18817).
- Tian, "Provable Scaling Laws of Feature Emergence from Learning Dynamics of Grokking" (Li_2 framework), arXiv 2509.21519 (v5) [ABS]. Stages: lazy -> independent feature learning -> interactive. During the lazy stage the backpropagated gradient G_F already "carries information about the target label"; v5 comment: "G_F carries useful signals also at initial stage". Emerging features are local maxima of an energy E — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21519).
- Varma, Shah, Kenton, Kramár, arXiv 2309.02390 (2023) [ABS]. Circuit-efficiency account; predicts ungrokking and semi-grokking — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02390).
- Minegishi, Iwasawa, Matsuo, arXiv 2310.19470, TMLR [ABS]. "Grokked tickets" (masks from the generalizing phase) remove the delay on modular arithmetic, polynomial regression, sparse parity, MNIST; good subnetworks show periodic weight patterns and rapid structural change at generalization — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19470).

### Inferences
- The published 2026 early-warning signals (spectral entropy, ILDR, commutator defect, 2RDM, DMD residual) typically lead the jump by ~1k steps, i.e. on the order of 5–10% of time-to-jump for a ~14k-step run (ILDR: 9–73% of budget). F1 (top W_E frequencies at epoch 60–100 vs. jump at 3,400–11,100) is a much earlier signal (~0.5–3% of time-to-jump) — if it holds up, it is earlier than any lead time reported in these papers, and of a different type (identity of features, not timing).
- F1 is consistent with Nanda's three-phase picture but sits *inside* Nanda's "memorization" phase (0–1.4k), earlier than where Nanda reports measurable circuit formation; Nanda's Gini is flat in that window, so a Gini-type sparsity metric would not show it — the signal is in the *ranking* of frequency norms, not their concentration.
- F1 is the embedding-level analogue of Barak et al.'s "Fourier gap": the population gradient favours specific Fourier components from the start, and this is invisible in loss/accuracy.
- The neuron-FVE timeline in F1 (0.16 @ep3000, 0.6 @ep7000) matches the spectral-entropy paper's observation that representation Fourier alignment builds gradually through the pre-jump phase rather than at the jump; the "embedding first, MLP neurons later" ordering is a new observation relative to these papers.

### Gaps
- No paper found that reports quantitative prediction accuracy (AUROC, hit rate) for a signal available in the first ~1–2% of training, other than Notsawo (qualitative only, first 400 steps).
- Could not verify Clauw et al.'s lead-time numbers or Fan et al.'s rank-vs-jump timing beyond abstracts.
- Singular-learning-theory local learning coefficient (LLC) as a stage detector (Hoogland et al., Lau et al.) was not checked in this session; worth verifying as an additional progress-measure family.
- Doshi et al. (arXiv 2310.13061) use Fourier-based inverse participation ratio of neuron weights in corrupted-label modular arithmetic [ABS — abstract does not mention timing]; whether they track IPR as an early predictor is unverified — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.13061).

## Q2. Where grokking / delayed generalization has been shown beyond modular arithmetic

### Takeaway
Delayed generalization is now documented in images (MNIST, CIFAR-10, Imagenette), molecules (QM9), sentiment/text classification, hierarchical syntax, multi-hop factual reasoning (synthetic and real KG data), and, since 2025–2026, in one-pass LLM pretraining (OLMoE; BLiMP grammatical generalization in pretraining). Most non-toy demonstrations require engineered conditions (large init, scaled-down data, data augmentation) or redefine "train vs. test" (exposure-based proxy splits). No dedicated LoRA/fine-tuning grokking paper was found.

### Cited Findings
- Liu, Michaud, Tegmark, "Omnigrok: Grokking Beyond Algorithmic Data", arXiv 2210.01117, ICLR 2023 [ABS]. "LU mechanism" (train loss L-shaped, test loss U-shaped vs. weight norm); induces grokking on images (MNIST), language (IMDb) and molecules (QM9) — via large initialisation; eliminates grokking on algorithmic data by constraining norm — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.01117). (Specific datasets MNIST/IMDb/QM9 are from the paper body/common citation, not the abstract.)
- Humayun et al., arXiv 2402.15555, ICML 2024 [ABS]. Grokking "much more widespread": CNN on CIFAR-10, ResNet on Imagenette; delayed adversarial robustness — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.15555).
- Fan, Pascanu, Jaggi, arXiv 2405.19454 [ABS]. Deep (12-layer) MLPs grok more than shallow ones, multi-stage generalization — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.19454).
- Kumar et al., arXiv 2310.06110 [ABS]. Lazy-to-rich account extends to MNIST, one-layer transformers, student–teacher — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06110).
- Golechha, arXiv 2405.12755 [ABS]. Grokking in real-world classification with cross-entropy, outside expected weight-norm ranges — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.12755).
- Prakash & Martin, arXiv 2506.04434 / 2602.02859 [ABS]. 3-layer MLP on 1k-sample MNIST groks and later "anti-groks" — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04434).
- Zhu, Fu, Zhou, Lin, "Critical Data Size of Language Models from a Grokking Perspective", arXiv 2401.10463 (2024) [ABS]. Grokking reproduced on simple language models by rescaling init and wd; generalization only above a critical data size, which grows with model size; transitions smoother on language data — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10463).
- Murty, Sharma, Andreas, Manning, "Grokking of Hierarchical Structure in Vanilla Transformers", arXiv 2305.18741, ACL 2023 [ABS]. "Structural grokking": hierarchical generalization emerges after training far beyond in-domain saturation; inverted-U in depth; the optimal depth is identified by the tree-structuredness metric — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18741).
- Wang, Yue, Su, Sun, "Grokked Transformers are Implicit Reasoners", arXiv 2405.15071, NeurIPS 2024 [ABS]. Implicit composition and comparison reasoning over parametric knowledge learned "only through grokking"; generalizing-circuit formation tied to relative efficiency of generalizing vs. memorizing circuits; OOD systematicity fails for composition, succeeds for comparison; a fully grokked transformer beats GPT-4-Turbo / Gemini-1.5-Pro on a large-search-space task — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15071). (The inferred/atomic ratio threshold reported in the paper was not verified here.)
- Abramov, Steinbauer, Kasneci, "Grokking in the Wild: Data Augmentation for Real-World Multi-Hop Reasoning with Transformers", arXiv 2504.20752, ICML 2025 [ABS]. First extension to real-world factual data: augment KGs with synthetic data to push inferred/atomic ratio φ_r above the grokking threshold; even factually incorrect synthetic data helps; up to 95–100% on 2WikiMultiHopQA — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.20752).
- Li, Fan, Zhou, "Grokking in LLM Pretraining? Monitor Memorization-to-Generalization without Test", arXiv 2506.21551, ICLR 2026 [ABS]. First study in practical one-epoch pretraining: grokking occurs in OLMoE (7B MoE) checkpoints; different data groups grok asynchronously ("local grokking"); MoE routing pathways evolve from random/instance-specific to structured/transferable after loss converges; two zero-cost training-data metrics (pathway similarity across samples; expert consistency across layers) track downstream generalization without test sets — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21551). LoRA instruction tuning is used only as an evaluation probe [SNIP] — [search result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.21551v2).
- Muckatira, Shivagunde, Deshpande, Rumshisky, "A Pre-Training Analogue of Grokking in Language Models: Tracing Delayed Grammatical Generalization", arXiv 2606.00230, AACL-IJCNLP 2026 Main [ABS]. Exposure-based framework: BLiMP pairs whose "critical phrase" occurs in the pretraining window form a proxy-train split, the rest proxy-validation; delayed generalization across 5 grammatical phenomena; after generalization, grammatical concept vectors are more predictive and occupy a *higher*-dimensional subspace; critical-token attention concentrated in few heads — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00230), [ACL Anthology PDF](https://aclanthology.org/2026.aacl-long.213.pdf).
- Xu, arXiv 2602.16967 [ABS]. Grokking-style delayed generalization on SCAN compositional generalization and Dyck-1 depth prediction — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16967).
- Carvalho et al., "Grokking Explained: A Statistical Phenomenon", arXiv 2502.01774 (2025) [ABS]. Argues train/test distribution shift (e.g. via imbalanced sub-category sampling) is the key factor; grokking can occur with dense data — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.01774).
- Olsson et al., "In-context Learning and Induction Heads", arXiv 2209.11895 (2022) [ABS]. Related phase change (not grokking per se): induction heads form at the same point as a sharp rise in in-context learning, visible as a bump in training loss — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.11895). The 2RDM paper uses induction-head formation as a test bed for its early-warning signal [ABS] — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29805).
- Non-neural grokking: RFM/kernel machines with AGOP grok modular arithmetic — [arXiv 2407.20199](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20199) [ABS].

### Inferences
- Outside algorithmic data, the early-monitoring metrics that exist are model-internal and task-agnostic (MoE pathway consistency, concept-vector predictivity, local complexity, feature rank, HTSR α). None reads out *the identity of the eventual feature basis* early, because in these settings there is no known closed-form target basis analogous to Fourier frequencies.
- A transferable version of F1 would need a task with a known target basis (group representations for S5 / non-abelian groups, multiplicative characters for a·b mod p per 2606.17399, tree structure for Murty's tasks) — these are the natural next testbeds.

### Gaps
- No paper found specifically on grokking during LoRA / parameter-efficient fine-tuning of pretrained LLMs (search returned only pretraining papers that use LoRA as an eval probe).
- Exact numbers for Omnigrok (init scale, epochs), Wang et al. (φ threshold, steps to grok) and Murty et al. (steps) were not verified beyond abstracts.

## Q3. Has anyone shown that the final features are readable from the embedding very early (first ~1–2% of training) and used this as a predictor?

### Takeaway
Not in standard IID-trained modular addition, as far as this search found. Theory (2026) predicts it — each neuron's winning frequency is set by its initial spectral magnitude and phase ("lottery ticket") — and one non-peer-reviewed 2026 preprint shows early embedding-frequency locking under *fixed (non-IID) data ordering*, but nobody reports a seed-level test of "top early W_E frequencies predict final key frequencies" or uses it as a predictor. F1 appears to fill this gap.

### Cited Findings
- He, Wang, Chen, Yang, "On the Mechanism and Dynamics of Modular Addition: Fourier Features, Lottery Ticket, and Grokking", arXiv 2602.16849 (Feb 2026) [FT, first ~40% of text]. Gradient-flow proof that "frequencies compete within each neuron, with the 'winner' determined by its initial spectral magnitude and phase alignment"; Observation 6: the frequency "with the largest α(0) and β(0) and the smallest misalignment D(0) ultimately wins". Proven only under a special single-frequency-per-neuron initialisation; for general random init the account is qualitative. Empirics: 2-layer bias-free net, M=512, **p=23**, ReLU, AdamW lr 1e-4; grokking run with train fraction 0.75, wd 2.0; memorization by ~step 1,000, second generalization phase starts ~step 10,000. Fig. 4 shows a single-neuron illustration and a contour of final vs. initial magnitude — **no prediction accuracy, no seed count, no early-embedding readout test** — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16849).
- Wang, Yao, Liu, Lu, "Probability-Signature Dynamics: Unpacking Modular Addition Learning Within Two-Layer Networks", arXiv 2610.11833 (Oct 2026, submitted to ICLR 2027) [ABS]. Leading gradient interactions for modular addition are cyclic shift operators diagonalised by the DFT, giving approximately decoupled Fourier-mode dynamics that explain Fourier sparsity, frequency matching and phase alignment from the start of training — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11833).
- Tian, arXiv 2509.21519 [ABS]. Backpropagated signal G_F carries label structure already in the lazy/initial stage, letting hidden nodes learn features independently before interaction — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21519).
- LeDoux, "The Order Is The Message", arXiv 2603.25047 (Mar 2026, single author, preprint; 51 pp.) [FT via PDF text]. Modular arithmetic with **p=9973**, 0.3% training fraction, *fixed* data orderings: STRIDE ordering — fundamental F=101 "becomes the peak frequency in the embedding spectrum by epoch 3 and crosses the 10x significance threshold by epoch 14", harmonics appear sequentially (202 @ep21, 404 @ep39, 808 @ep61); "by epoch 30 the spectral structure of the eventual solution is already visible"; 99.5% test acc by epoch 487. FIXED-RANDOM ordering — "peak embedding frequency locks to F = 2205 by epoch 7, the initial spectral dominant of its particular random permutation", but by the end F=3234 "surpassed F = 2205 as the highest-power frequency" (so the early peak was not the final top frequency); 99.5% by epoch 659. IID baseline: 0.30% test acc after 5,000 epochs (did not grok in budget), so **no early-frequency analysis exists for the IID grokking case** in this paper. Author frames early epochs as a "critical learning period" for a feature — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25047).
- AlquBoj/AlQuabeh, Bojkovic, Nwadike, "Mechanistic Insights into Grokking from the Embedding Layer", arXiv 2505.15624 (May 2025) [ABS]. Embeddings are central to grokking: adding embeddings to MLPs induces the delay; rare tokens stagnate under sparse updates + wd; bilinear embedding×downstream coupling creates saddles and sensitivity to init; embedding-specific LR ratio η_E/η_W ∝ σ_max(E)/σ_max(W)·f_W/f_E accelerates grokking — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15624). This supports "embedding moves first / downstream weights lag" but does not test early frequency identity.
- Truong et al., arXiv 2604.13123 [FT]. Fourier alignment of the penultimate representation already rises 0.12 -> ~0.25 by ~step 200 (end of memorization) out of a ~14k-step time-to-grok; embedding frequencies not tracked — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.13123).
- Nanda et al., arXiv 2301.05217 [FT]. Final key frequencies differ by seed; W_E sparsity analysed only at the end; Gini flat during memorization — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.05217).
- Minegishi et al., arXiv 2310.19470 [ABS]. Masks taken *after* grokking ("grokked tickets") applied from init remove the delay — evidence that the generalizing subnetwork is compatible with the initial weights, but derived post hoc, not predicted early — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19470).
- Zhong, Liu, Tegmark, Andreas, "The Clock and the Pizza", arXiv 2306.17844, NeurIPS 2023 [ABS]. Small changes to hyperparameters and **initialisations** change which algorithm (Clock vs. Pizza) is found — init matters for the discovered solution — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.17844).
- Morwani, Edelman, Oncescu, Zhao, Kakade, arXiv 2311.07568, ICLR 2024 spotlight [ABS]. Margin maximization fully specifies that Fourier features are used, but (as an end-state theory) does not say which frequencies a given seed picks — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.07568).
- Nguyen, "The Discrete-Log Clock", arXiv 2606.17399, ICML 2026 MI workshop [ABS]. For a·b mod 113, analysing in the multiplicative-character basis gives sparse embedding spectrum (Gini 0.58 vs 0.07 in additive basis), 4 key frequencies, 96.9% of MLP neurons single-frequency — the correct basis is a prerequisite for any F1-style readout on other operations — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17399).

### Inferences
- Positioning of F1: (1) it is the first (found) empirical, seed-level confirmation in the standard IID Nanda setup that the eventual key frequencies are already the dominant W_E Fourier components at ~0.5–3% of time-to-jump — the empirical counterpart of He et al.'s lottery-ticket theorem (which they only illustrate on single neurons, p=23, 2-layer MLP) and Barak et al.'s "Fourier gap"; (2) it predicts *which* features, whereas every published early-warning metric predicts *whether/when*; (3) the 4/5-seeds, ">=3 of final key frequencies in top-4" result should be compared against a chance baseline (choosing 4 of 56 nonzero frequencies for p=113; expected overlap with ~5 key frequencies ≈ 4·5/56 ≈ 0.36), which would make the effect very unlikely by chance — this calculation is my own, not from a paper.
- LeDoux's FIXED-RANDOM result (early peak later overtaken) is a cautionary counterexample: early top-1 can be displaced, which argues for reporting top-k overlap (as F1 does) rather than top-1.
- The "embedding commits early, MLP neurons commit near the jump" split is consistent with AlquBoj et al.'s bilinear-coupling/saddle story and with Tian's lazy -> independent -> interactive stages, but no paper reports this layer-wise ordering of commitment in time.
- A natural predictor follow-up suggested by the literature: combine the early W_E frequency readout (which features) with a timing signal (spectral entropy, ILDR, Notsawo oscillations) to forecast both which frequencies and when.
